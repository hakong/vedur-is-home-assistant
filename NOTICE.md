# Unofficial Community Integration

Icelandic Met Office Weather (`vedur_is`) is an independently developed,
unofficial Home Assistant custom integration. It is not created, maintained,
approved, or endorsed by the Icelandic Meteorological Office (IMO), and is
not affiliated with that organisation.

The IMO provides the underlying weather data through vedur.is and gottvedur.is.
That data attribution does not imply that the IMO provides or supports this
software. Report integration issues to this repository, not to the IMO:
https://github.com/hakong/vedur-is-home-assistant/issues

The integration's custom icon and wordmark identify this community project.
They are not the IMO logo, do not incorporate the IMO windrose, and do not
indicate official status or approval. Names and trademarks referenced by this
project remain the property of their respective owners.

Weather data remains subject to the provider's terms:
https://en.vedur.is/about-imo/the-web/conditions

This attribution and non-affiliation notice does not grant permission to use
the IMO's trademarks or change the provider's data terms. See [BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md)
for the official logo restriction and the provenance of this project's artwork.
